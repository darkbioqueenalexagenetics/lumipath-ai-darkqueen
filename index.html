Our trust system

Honest about what we know — and what we don't

✓ Verified

Confirmed from official source

Deadline, eligibility, and coverage confirmed directly from the official government or university portal.

~ Partially verified

Some details unclear

Scholarship exists on an official page but one or more details could not be fully confirmed. Manual check recommended.

🧠 AI Analysis

Generated insight — not a fact

AI-generated based on patterns. Useful as a starting point but must be verified independently before applying.

! Unverified

Could not confirm

No reliable source found. Do not rely on this for application decisions. Use it only as a lead for your own research.
