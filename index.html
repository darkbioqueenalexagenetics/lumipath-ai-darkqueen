Our trust system

Honest about what we know — and what we don't

Every piece of information on LumiPath carries a trust label. No platform does this — we do, because students deserve honesty.

Verified
Confirmed from official source
This scholarship's deadline, eligibility, and coverage were confirmed directly from the official government or university portal. Safe to rely on — always double-check before applying.
Partially verified
Some details unclear
The scholarship exists and was found on an official page, but one or more details — like the deadline or coverage — could not be fully confirmed. Manual verification recommended.
AI analysis
Generated insight — not a fact
This response was generated by AI based on patterns and known information. It may be useful as a starting point but must not be treated as confirmed data. Always verify independently.
Unverified
AI could not confirm this
No reliable source was found to confirm this information. Do not rely on this entry for application decisions. Use it only as a lead for your own further research.
🤝
The LumiPath AI honesty rule
Our AI is trained with one strict rule: if it is not sure, it must say so clearly. It will never hallucinate scholarship deadlines, invent eligibility rules, or present guesses as facts.
"I could not verify this information. Please check the official source before making any application decisions."
